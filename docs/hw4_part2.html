The same wheel on five stocks

Not required by the assignment. The plain wheel of Part I, run on all five stocks the screen picked at once.
Periods2024-01-02 → 2025-12-31 · 2026-01-02 → 2026-10-16StocksMA · APA · V · FISV · ADIStarting cash5 × $100,000DataDaily closes · LSEG
Motivation

The largest losses in this project came from a single stock falling while the wheel held it: Fiserv's wheel lost −48.7% in 2024-2025. Hence, the same wheel was run on all five stocks the mean-reversion screen picked, side by side, to see whether spreading it across names reduces that risk. The setup was committed (a96ec04) before it was run, and nothing in Part I was chosen from its result.

Five plain wheels, each with $100,000 at its stock's committed setting and run exactly as in Part I, with their daily account values added into one $500,000 portfolio. The benchmarks are the same five stocks bought and held, $100,000 each, and T-bills.

What it shows
  • Less risk for the same return: The portfolio's return is the average of the five by construction (+28.8% in 2024-2025), but its volatility was 9.8% against 15.5% for a single wheel on average, and its maximum drawdown −14.5% against −26.3%.
  • Why: The five wheels' monthly results barely moved together, with a median correlation of 0.12 in 2024-2025 and −0.09 in 2026. Specifically, a wheel loses when its own stock falls through the strike, and that happened at different times for different stocks.
  • One collapse, absorbed: Fiserv's wheel lost −48.7% in 2024-2025, which cost the portfolio about a fifth as much.
  • Sharpe ratio: 0.88 in 2024-2025, against 0.55 had the five moved in lockstep (1.55 against 0.99 in 2026). Though the average of the five single-stock Sharpe ratios (1.43) is higher, averaging Sharpe ratios gives a calm book such as Mastercard's the same weight as a volatile one, so it is not the right comparison.
  • Against holding the stocks: In 2024-2025 the portfolio returned +28.8% against +8.4% for holding the five (Sharpe 0.88 against 0.04). In 2026 it returned +14.0% against +28.4% (Sharpe 1.55 against 1.59), because the covered calls capped a strong rally.
