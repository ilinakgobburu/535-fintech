The put-tilted trend condor

A weekly iron condor on NVDA, sold only when its options look expensive against NVDA's own recent volatility. The sold call sits farther out than the sold put, and a trigger hedges the direction away once NVDA moves more than one weekly standard deviation from the entry. The rules were registered before any of this strategy's results were computed. The weekly test on 2024–26 passed its bar; a 52-week forward walk on weeks that have not happened yet starts after the code freeze.

1 · Thesis

What the strategy is for

The strategy takes no view on the direction of NVDA's price, only on its volatility: the argument is that NVDA's weekly options are priced for larger moves than the stock subsequently makes. This is expected because option sellers demand compensation for bearing NVDA's jump and tail risk, so implied volatility tends to exceed realized volatility, and a defined-risk iron condor that is hedged once a move becomes large can collect that premium without depending on where the price goes.

2 · The rules

The rules, with their parameters

One condor a week on NVDA, opened only when its options look expensive, with the sold call farther out than the sold put; the headline also hedges once NVDA moves far.

The weekly NVDA condor

As registered
Schedule
Each week on NVDA, decided at the close of the week's first session and held to that week's expiry.
The gate
Trade only when the condor's edge is positive, otherwise nothing that week. The edge is how much more the condor's options can be sold for than they are worth: for each option sold, its bid less its fair value, and for each option bought, its fair value less its ask. Fair value is priced at NVDA's realized volatility over the last 21 sessions, so a positive edge means the options look expensive even after paying the bid-ask spread.
Condor and tilt
A defined-risk iron condor inside four risk limits, the sold put's chance of finishing in the money at most 0.20 and the sold call's at most 0.10, so the call sits farther out.
The trigger
Headline only: once NVDA moves more than one weekly standard deviation from the entry close, hedge to delta-neutral and stay neutral to the expiry.
Judged fill
Every option at the entry close's bid (a sale) or ask (a purchase), 5 bp on every share trade; cash earns the T-bill.
Full rules as registered, with every parameter
Schedule
Every week on NVDA, decided at the close of the week's first session (a Monday, or the Tuesday after a Monday holiday) and held into that week's expiry (the Friday, or the Thursday before a Friday holiday).
Size
One $100,000 book: n = floor($100,000 ÷ (100 × NVDA's close)) contracts on every leg.
The gate
Trade only when the condor's edge is positive, otherwise nothing that week. The edge adds, for each sold leg, its bid less its fair value, and for each bought leg, its fair value less its ask, times the shares; fair value is the Black-76 option-pricing formula's value. The value uses NVDA's realized volatility over the last 21 sessions, √rv21, on the options' calendar-day time basis: σ = √rv21 × √((h/252) ÷ (D/365)), with h the week's sessions after the entry and D its calendar days.
The condor
Sell a put and a call, buy a farther put and call (Kpw < Kp < S < Kc < Kcw): of every eligible four-strike choice, the one with the largest edge that passes four rails at the entry close: |net delta × S| ≤ $5,000; the loss on a one-day move of 3 daily standard deviations, down or up, ≤ $2,000; |net vega| ≤ $300 per vol point; maximum loss ≤ $10,000. An eligible option has a bid above 0 and a spread of at most 0.50 of its mid.
The tilt
The sold put's chance of finishing in the money is at most 0.20, the sold call's at most 0.10, so the call sits farther out than the put.
The trigger
Headline only. Unhedged at entry. At each close from the session after the entry to the session before the expiry, the trigger fires the first time NVDA's move since the entry close, |ln(close ÷ entry close)|, exceeds one weekly standard deviation, σ × √T: σ the implied vol at the strike nearest the entry close and T the week's calendar days ÷ 365, both fixed at entry. The book then goes neutral (shares to minus the options' net delta) and stays neutral: at any later close where |net delta × close| > $5,000, it hedges again. It resets at each entry.
Assignment
Under the judged expiry rule, a short option is assigned only at the expiry close, when NVDA closes through its strike (an assumption: NVDA's options are American-style, so a holder can exercise earlier). Assigned shares, and any hedge shares, are closed at the next open.
Judged fill
The stress fill: every option at the entry close, at the bid for a sale and the ask for a purchase, plus 5 bp on every share trade, hedges included. It stands for deciding and trading near the close (a live trader would start by 3:50 pm New York). Cash earns the T-bill.
Reported beside
The limit fill (a limit at the mid for the next session, filled only if that session's mid reaches it) and the mid fill; the course server's assignment rule (a short option assigned at any close once it is in the money).
The two versions
Headline: the tilt and the trigger. Tilt only: the tilt without the trigger.

Theta and gamma nearly cancel. The headline collected $50,518 of time decay and gave back $46,041 to gamma, the cost of NVDA's moves against the short options, leaving +$4,477; tilt only kept +$4,217 and the plain condor +$719.

Vega lost money in all three (−$11,039, −$11,273, −$5,862): on balance, implied volatility rose while the condors were short it. Together the volatility lines (theta, gamma and vega) lost money in every book: −$6,562 for the headline, −$7,056 for tilt only and −$5,143 for the plain condor.

Direction is where the tilted versions earned. Delta, the book's exposure to NVDA's moves, made +$7,997 for the headline and +$14,462 for tilt only: 79% and 85% of each one's P&L above the T-bill interest (+$10,090 and +$16,957). The plain condor's delta was −$2,063. The residual is +$11,062 and +$11,609 in the tilted versions and only +$242 in the plain condor, so it comes with the tilt; but it is what the daily greeks leave unexplained, not a measured effect of direction, so the direction reading rests on delta alone.

So this is not a pure volatility bet. It is a volatility strategy that does not forecast direction but is designed around NVDA's upward drift, and in the backtest most of its profit came from how it handled direction.

Dollars on each $100,000 book over the weekly test, stress fill, expiry rule, from the engine's daily decomposition: theta, gamma, vega and delta are each day's greeks times that day's change (vega with each option's own implied-volatility change); the residual is the rest of the day's P&L: moves beyond the second-order terms, the interaction of price and volatility moves, and the expiry day's settlement. Rows may not add exactly, due to rounding. Dollar P&L on a growing book and the results table's summed monthly returns differ: the headline's +$10,090 above the T-bill interest is +10.09% of the starting book, its summed monthly return above T-bills +8.91%.

The registration in full
Pre-registered
On November 23, 2026 (commit 53f1ae7), before any forward week was pulled or computed. One amendment, on November 23, 2026 (commit 53b1744), also before any forward week: each month is charged the T-bill only for the part of it the book ran, and a first entry with fewer than 5 sessions left in its month counts in the next month, so a 2026-11-30 start scores December 2026 to November 2027.
Versions
The headline and tilt only, exactly as in the weekly test, each its own fresh $100,000 book; same code, same engine.
Window
52 consecutive weekly entries from the first entry after the code freeze (expected 2026-11-30; the last expected 2027-11-22, expiry 2027-11-26). A week with no options served counts as one of the 52, with no trade.
Judged
After the last week settles: each version's pooled monthly stress Sharpe (expiry rule) over the months it covers, at least 0.5, the weekly test's bar and measure.
Nothing changes
No change to the strategy, the engine, the books, the window or the bar once it starts; a change would be a new version with its own weeks. No early stop: the weekly figures can be read but decide nothing.
Status
Not started. The code freeze is not committed yet; the first scored entry is the first weekly entry whose close comes after it, expected 2026-11-30.
