Method & honest caveats

  • Sparsity here is a lower bound. A "listed series" is a (date, RIC) carrying at least one of the two fields. A contract that was listed but had neither a quote nor a print leaves no trace in the pull, so it cannot appear in the denominator. The real holes are at least this bad, probably worse.
  • The sheet stops where the data stops. Interpolation is Delaunay-linear, which returns nothing outside the convex hull of the observations, and grid nodes further than $1.25 in strike-space from any real observation are blanked as well. A wide interior hole stays a hole instead of becoming a smooth ramp.
  • Every view is computed once, in Python. The browser picks a precomputed slice rather than recomputing it, so this page and the Reflex app cannot disagree about a number.
  • Color is never the only encoding. The mark draws as a circle, the trade as a diamond. The palette was checked against the plot surface with a contrast and colour-vision validator rather than chosen by eye.
